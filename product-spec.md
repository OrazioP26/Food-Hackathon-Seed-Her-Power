# Seed Cycling Millet Crackers

Updated ingredients, calories and accompaniments.

## 1. Product Overview

**Target consumer:** Busy, health-conscious women seeking convenient, nutrient-dense snacks.

**Product:** Maple-cinnamon millet crackers with phase-specific seed combinations, paired with a choice of four jams or dips.

**28-Day Subscription:**
* 10 Phase 1 cracker packs (flax + pumpkin)
* 10 Phase 2 cracker packs (sunflower + sesame)
* 20 individually packaged jam or dip cups
* 4 accompaniment flavors, with 5 cups of each flavor

**Serving target:** 40 g cracker ingredient-equivalent portion + 20 g jam or dip.

## 6. Four Jam and Dip Options

All accompaniments are intended to be individually packaged in 20 g (0.71 oz) cups.

| Flavor | Main Ingredients | Estimated Calories per 20 g |
|---|---|---|
| Blueberry-Lemon Jam | Blueberries, sugar, lemon juice, pectin | ~50 kcal |
| Strawberry-Raspberry Jam | Strawberries, raspberries, sugar, lemon juice, pectin | ~50 kcal |
| Cinnamon Almond Butter Dip | Almond butter, cinnamon, salt | ~120 kcal |
| Vanilla Sunflower Butter Dip | Sunflower seed butter, vanilla powder, salt | ~120 kcal |

* **Blueberry-Lemon Jam:** Tart and fruity, balancing the crackers' maple sweetness.
* **Strawberry-Raspberry Jam:** Familiar, sweet-tart flavor with broad consumer appeal.
* **Cinnamon Almond Butter Dip:** Creamy and rich, contributing unsaturated fats and some additional protein.
* **Vanilla Sunflower Butter Dip:** Nutty, creamy, and complementary to both seed combinations.

> **Important:** These are proposed ingredient lists and estimated calories, not validated shelf-stable recipes. Commercial shelf-stable formulations or professionally validated processing and packaging will be required. Almond butter introduces a tree-nut allergen; sesame is already present in Phase 2 crackers.

## 9. Nutritional and Marketing Positioning

**Primary message:** Natural, convenient fuel for busy, health-conscious women.

**Key nutritional features:**
* Whole-grain millet and seeds
* Plant-based protein and dietary fiber
* Mostly unsaturated fats
* Magnesium, zinc, and other essential minerals
* Two seed combinations inspired by traditional seed cycling
* Ready-to-eat, individually portioned snacks

**Important tradeoffs:**
* The butter dips increase total snack calories to approximately 305–311.
* Conventional shelf-stable jams may contribute significant added sugar.
* Energy claims should focus on providing nutritious fuel, not proven prevention of hormonal energy crashes.
* Seed cycling has not been clinically established as a way to balance hormones.

## 10. Product Messaging

**Natural energy, made for your cycle.**

Nutrient-dense millet crackers paired with delicious jams and creamy dips, designed for busy women who want convenient, wholesome fuel throughout their menstrual cycle.

Two seed blends. Four flavors. Zero prep.
