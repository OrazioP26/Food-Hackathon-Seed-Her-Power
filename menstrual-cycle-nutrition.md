# Menstrual Cycle Nutrition

Key nutrients for each phase of the menstrual cycle.

## Menstrual Phase (Days 1–5)

* **Iron:** Supports red blood cell production and replenishes stores lost from menstruation. [1]
* **Magnesium:** Helps relax uterine muscles and ease pelvic cramping. [1]
* **B-Complex Vitamins:** Assist with cellular energy metabolism when fatigue peaks. [1]

## Follicular Phase (Days 6–14)

* **Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10):** Supports cellular energy and egg health as estrogen begins to rise. [1]
* **B-Vitamins:** Help maintain steady energy production and support rising metabolic demands. [1]

## Ovulatory Phase (Days 15–16)

* **Zinc:** Supports hormonal production and natural immune/antioxidant defenses. [1, 2]
* **Vitamin D:** Aids mood regulation and general immune or inflammatory response. [1, 2]

## Luteal Phase (Days 17–28)

* **Calcium (500–1,200 mg):** Shown to help alleviate premenstrual mood changes, fatigue, and food cravings. [1]
* **Vitamin B6:** Supports neurotransmitters and emotional well-being, often paired with magnesium. [1, 2]
* **Magnesium (300 mg):** Assists with irritability, anxiety, and sleep disturbances common before a period. [1]
